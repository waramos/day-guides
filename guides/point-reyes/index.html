Point Reyes & Tomales Bay

A spontaneous West Marin day from Alameda: oysters, local soft serve, dramatic coast, and two different ways to sequence the adventure.

Plan B timeline

The practical late-start day

Times are deliberately approximate—traffic on Highway 1 and West Marin roads can move them around. The sequence is the important part.

~3:00 PM

Leave Alameda

Start near Sherman Street & Santa Clara Avenue and head north toward Marin. Don’t add extra detours yet.

~4:30–5

Tony’s Seafood · Marshall

Make oysters the anchor meal. Tony’s, part of the Hog Island family, lists Saturday/Sunday summer hours of 11:30 AM–7:30 PM. Reservations and walk-ins are accepted.

DirectionsOfficial page
~5:45–6

Palace Market · Point Reyes Station

Get the famous soft serve and wander the tiny downtown for a few minutes. The market currently describes it as Valley Ford Jersey Milk soft serve; it replaced the longtime buffalo-milk version in March 2026.

DirectionsMarket site
~6:20

Head for Drakes Beach

NPS describes Drakes Beach as a broad, easily accessed beach backed by sandstone cliffs, with generally smaller waves than Point Reyes’ west-facing beaches.

DirectionsNPS info
7:34 PM

Sunset

September 5 sunset is about 7:34 PM at Point Reyes National Seashore. Arrive early enough to park, layer up and actually enjoy the light rather than watching it through the windshield.

Tomales Bay oysters

Palace Market soft serve

Drakes Beach

Optional variation

Want the lighthouse instead?

If the lighthouse itself is the priority, go there before food. Current NPS weekend hours put the stair closure at one hour before sunset—roughly 6:34 PM today—and the visitor center stays open another 30 minutes, unless high winds close the stairs. The observation deck above the stairs is open much later.

That version means giving up The Marshall Store today and makes a full sit-down oyster meal afterward much tighter.

Quick facts

Lighthouse weekend access

Saturday/Sunday: visitor center 10 AM–sunset; stairs close one hour before sunset. Wind above 40 mph can close the stairs.

The Marshall Store

Saturday/Sunday regular hours: 11 AM–4 PM. No reservations; first come, first served.

Tony’s Seafood

Summer Saturday/Sunday hours: 11:30 AM–7:30 PM. Reservations accepted; walk-ins welcome.
